    Where is your data?

    An honest answer, no asterisks.

    On your phone

    • Records and receipt photos are stored on your phone.
    • Manual logging and notifications work without internet.
    • Backup to your own Google Drive, whenever you want.

    Leaves the phone, only if you use it

    • AI and statement scan send text to an AI provider, after you agree.
    • Shared wallets live on a server so you can open them together.
    • Currency rates need internet.

    No ads. Your data is not sold. Read the Privacy Policy

      Common questions

      Is Artosku free?

      You can use it for free. Pro and Max plans give a bigger AI allowance, and Business Mode is in Max.

      Does it work without internet?

      Manual logging, notifications, and reports work offline. AI, statement scan, shared wallets, and currency rates need internet.

      What leaves my phone?

      Records and receipt photos stay on the phone. Text you ask AI to read goes to an AI provider after you agree. Shared wallets are stored on a server. A Google Drive backup goes into your own Drive account.

      Are there ads?

      No ads, and your data is not sold.

      How do I install it?

      Search for Artosku on Google Play, or use the download button on this page.

      Is there an iPhone version?

      Android only for now.
