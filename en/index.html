FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Artosku free?
Yes, completely free. There's no subscription fee.
Is my financial data safe?
Your main financial data is stored on your phone. You can lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint, and make an encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.
How do I install Artosku?
Artosku is available directly on Google Play. Just open the download link on this page or search for "Artosku" on Google Play, then install it like any other app.
Can I use it on an iPhone?
For now, Artosku is only available for Android. iOS support is still being considered.
