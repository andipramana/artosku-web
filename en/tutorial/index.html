Artosku Guide

Short steps for using Artosku. Your data stays on your own phone.

Start here Recording money Wallets, categories & merchants Budgets & allowance Debts & installments Savings, assets & shopping Reports Sharing with others Backup & restore AI Chat & AI Streak & mascot Settings & account Quick how-tos for common goals Business Mode

Start here

Four steps, about five minutes.

  1. Set up a wallet

    A wallet is where your money lives: cash, a bank account or an e-wallet. On Home, if you have no wallet yet, tap Create Samples. Cash, Bank and E-Wallet wallets are made at once, and you can edit or delete them later. Want to make your own? Tap Create Your Own.

  2. Record your first transaction

    With AI: tap the + button at the bottom middle, then Record with AI. Say or type, e.g. "lunch 25k". Check the result, then save.

    By hand: tap +, pick Expense, Income or Transfer. Fill in the Amount, pick the Wallet and the Category, then tap Save.

  3. See the result on Home

    Go back to Home. Your total balance has changed. The daily allowance shows up here once you have made one (see the next step).

  4. What next

    Try Daily allowance (how much you can spend per day until payday), Budgets (a limit per category) and Backup to Google Drive (a copy of your data in your own Drive).

Recording money

Record transactions

Record money in and out with the + button in the middle. Pick the wallet, category and merchant.

  1. Tap the + button at the bottom middle, then pick Expense, Income or Transfer.
  2. Fill in the Amount, pick the Wallet and the Category (the merchant can stay empty).
  3. Tap Save.

Record with AI

Say or type "lunch 25k" and the AI prepares the transaction for you to check first.

  1. Tap the + button › Record with AI.
  2. Say or type the transaction, e.g. "lunch 25k with gopay". Several at once is fine.
  3. Check the result (amount, wallet, category) and tap Edit if something is off.
  4. Save.

Scan receipts

Photograph a receipt and the items and total fill in. Shipping, fees and discounts are read too.

  1. Tap the + button › Scan receipt.
  2. Pick the wallet, then Take Photo or Gallery (several receipts are fine).
  3. Tap Process N Receipts and wait until the draft is ready.
  4. Check the items and categories, then save.

Add many transactions at once

Enter several transactions on one screen, or split one receipt over several categories.

  1. Tap the + button › Add many.
  2. Pick the Expense, Income or Transfer tab.
  3. Fill in row by row (date, wallet, amount, category). For one like an earlier one use Copy old transaction.
  4. Tap Save All.

Import a statement from PDF

Read a bank statement PDF into a list of transactions you can check and save.

  1. Open Transactions, tap the ⋮ menu › Import statement.
  2. Pick the wallet the statement belongs to.
  3. Pick the PDF or CSV statement file from the bank, then tap Process File.
  4. Check the list on the Statement Review page (category, date, amount), then Save All.

Capture from notifications

Bank and e-wallet notifications on your phone become draft transactions to approve. Android only.

  1. Open More › Catat Notif, tap Allow notification access and turn it on for Artosku.
  2. Tap Choose bank and wallet apps and pick the apps to watch.
  3. Tap Connect to a wallet so drafts go to the right wallet.
  4. When a bank or e-wallet notification comes in, a draft appears on the Catat Notif page. Check it, then Record all.

Watched bank apps and clearing notification drafts

Choose the bank apps that are watched, connect wallets, turn on new draft alerts, and delete drafts or notification data.

  1. Open More › Capture notifications, then tap Settings (the icon at the top right).
  2. Under Monitored apps tap Manage to pick the bank and wallet apps. Turn on Tell me about new drafts for an alert.
  3. Drafts not showing? Tap Not coming in? then Allow again for notification access.
  4. To clean up, tap Delete all drafts or Delete all notification data.

Transfer fee

Record the bank admin fee together with the transfer: one card, and the source wallet balance is right.

  1. Tap the + button › Transfer (or edit an existing transfer).
  2. Fill in the Amount, From wallet and To wallet.
  3. Fill in Transfer fee (optional), or tap 2,500 or 6,500. Empty or 0 means no fee.
  4. Pick the Fee category, then Save. The fee is recorded as an expense and shows on one card with its transfer.

Copy an old transaction

Record a similar transaction again in one tap, without typing it from scratch.

  1. Open an old transaction in the Transactions tab, then tap the Copy icon.
  2. Or in Add many, tap Copy old transaction and pick it (you can search and filter).
  3. Change what you need (the date is today by default), then save.

Combined receipt (many categories)

One shopping receipt becomes one transaction with its items and each item's category.

  1. Tap the + button › Scan receipt, then photograph the receipt. In the result pick Combine into 1 receipt so it becomes one transaction with the item details.
  2. Or enter it yourself: in the transaction form turn on Split across several categories, then fill in each row. The rows must add up to the total.
  3. Save. The item details are kept inside the transaction.

Wallets, categories & merchants

Wallets

Manage cash, bank accounts and e-wallets and their balances. Move money between wallets.

  1. Open More › Wallet, then tap Add Wallet.
  2. Pick the type (bank, e-wallet, cash) or Add Manually.
  3. Fill in the Wallet name and the Opening balance, then Save.

Pockets

Split the balance of one wallet into several pockets, e.g. Emergency fund or Holiday.

  1. Open More › Wallet and tap the wallet.
  2. Under Pocket tap Add Pocket (New Pocket). Fill in the Pocket name and the Initial allocation, then Create Pocket.
  3. Change what is in it any time with the pocket's ⋮ menu › Manage funds: Add, Reduce or Transfer to another pocket.

Move money between pockets

Move money from one pocket to another pocket or wallet, recorded neatly.

  1. Open More › Wallet, tap the wallet, then open Pocket.
  2. Tap Pocket transfer (or Manage funds › Transfer).
  3. Pick From and To (a pocket or a wallet), enter the Amount, then tap Transfer.

Currency

Pick your main currency and record wallets or transactions in another one, rates update daily.

  1. Open More › Currency.
  2. Tap Main currency, then pick the new currency (Change the main currency).
  3. Every total, budget and report then uses it. Rates update automatically each day.

Categories

Organise income and expense categories, down to subcategories.

  1. Open More › Categories.
  2. Tap add for a new category (pick Expense or Income, a name, an icon).
  3. Tap a category to edit it, or hold and drag to reorder.

Category learning

Artosku remembers the category you chose for a description, and you can change it.

  1. Open More › Category Learning.
  2. See the keywords Artosku has learned from your habits.
  3. Edit or delete the ones that are off, so the next transactions land in the right category.

Merchants

See where money goes and comes from, with a default category for each place.

  1. Open More › Merchants.
  2. Tap add, enter the place name and its usual category.
  3. When recording, pick that merchant and the category fills itself in.

Budgets & allowance

Budgets

Set a spending limit per category each month and see what is left.

  1. Open More › Budget.
  2. Tap add, pick a category, pick the Period (Daily, Weekly or Monthly) and enter the limit.
  3. Artosku tells you when spending gets close to or passes the limit.
  4. Want to change the period (e.g. monthly to weekly)? Tap the budget, pick Daily, Weekly or Monthly while editing, then Save and Change. The old budget is replaced; other months or weeks are not touched.

Daily budget (a limit per category each day)

A spending limit per category for each day, with special rules for certain days (e.g. Friday or the weekend) or one date. Different from the Daily allowance, which is worked out for you from all your money.

  1. Open More › Budget, then tap the + button in the header.
  2. Pick the Category (e.g. Snacks), then Period: Daily. Fill in Limit per day (general, all days).
  3. For certain days tap + By day: pick the days (e.g. Sat and Sun), fill in the limit or turn on No limit, then Save. For one date tap + By date.
  4. What is used: date > day > general. Save. See it in the Daily tab (the ‹ › arrows move the day, up to 30 days ahead) or on the Today's budget card on Home.
  5. Different from the Daily allowance: the allowance is worked out for you from all your money until payday, the daily budget is a limit per category that you set yourself. You are reminded once when the limit of that day is passed.

Daily allowance

Work out how much you can spend per day until payday, with your mascot.

  1. Open More › Daily Allowance, then tap Create daily allowance.
  2. Enter the Allowance name (e.g. Snack allowance) and create it.
  3. Tap Set up calculation to choose which money counts: wallets or pockets, recurring bills, due debts, savings deposits, regular income.
  4. The result (how much you can spend per day until payday) shows on Home. Turn off Show on home if you do not want it.
  5. Want a limit per category each day that you set yourself? That is the Daily budget: More › Budget › the Daily tab.

Leave a transaction out of the allowance

A big purchase or money held for someone else does not use up the daily and weekly allowance. Choose per transaction: spread over the days that are left, or ignored completely. It can be undone at any time.

  1. One transaction: tap it (on Home or in History), then tap Leave out of allowance. If you have more than one allowance, pick the allowance first.
  2. Pick How to count: Spread over the days left (today's allowance is not used up at once, the allowance of the next days goes down a little) or Ignore completely (as if the transaction never happened, careful: the allowance can be bigger than the money you have). Then tap Leave out. An income that is left out is always Not counted: it adds nothing to the allowance.
  3. Many at once: More › Daily Allowance › pick your allowance › Excluded transactions › + Add transaction. Search note, category, merchant or amount, set 30 days / This month / Pick dates, tick the ones to leave out, tap Save · {n} selected, then pick How to count.
  4. Want to undo? In the transaction detail tap Undo exclusion. Or in the Excluded transactions list: tap the row then Release, swipe the row, or open + Add transaction, untick it and Save.
  5. It applies to both the Daily and the Weekly view. The number shows in the allowance formula as "{n} transactions left out".

Recurring transactions

Regular bills and subscriptions are recorded on their date, with reminders.

  1. Open More › Recurring Transactions, then tap Add recurring transaction.
  2. Pick Expense or Income (a transfer type is not available here), fill in the Amount, the wallet (down to its pocket), the Category and the Note.
  3. Pick the Frequency: Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly, then Start from the date and Remind how many days before.
  4. Save. Artosku records it automatically around 00:00 each time it falls due, or when you open Artosku, and reminds you. The note is dated the day it was due.

Report period (payday)

Choose when "this month" starts, for example on payday.

  1. Open More › Report Period.
  2. Turn on Custom period start date, then fill in Start date with your payday (a number 1-28, e.g. 25).
  3. "This month" in Transactions, Reports and Home then runs from that day. The date range shows under the field.
  4. Turn the switch off to go back to the normal calendar (the 1st to the end of the month).

Debts & installments

Debts & receivables

Record who borrowed, how much and when it is due. Due-date reminders, and it can be paid in parts.

  1. Open More › Debts & Receivables.
  2. Tap add, then choose whether you owe or someone owes you.
  3. Fill in the name, the amount, the Date (the day the money changed hands, it can be in the past but not the future) and the due date.
  4. Save. To pay, tap Paid off (receivable) or Pay (debt) in the same list and set the Payment date if it was not today.

Installments & Paylater

Record installments, credit and paylater bills so you know what is left and when it is due.

  1. Open More › Installments, then tap Add installment.
  2. Fill in the Installment name (e.g. Motorbike, Phone), Total, How many payments, the Installment payment period and the First installment date.
  3. Pick Taken from wallet and a Category, then Save. Installments are recorded automatically on each due date.
  4. Pay early or see what is left with the Pay button on the installment card.

Arisan (rotating savings)

Record your arisan: dues run automatically each period and the pot you receive is recorded on your turn.

  1. Open More › Installments, then tap Add installment.
  2. Enter the Installment name (e.g. Neighborhood arisan), then turn on This is an arisan.
  3. Fill in the Number of members (how many payments), Dues each period, the Installment payment period and the First installment date.
  4. Fill in Your turn to receive the pot (which payment), then pick Taken from wallet and the Category of the dues.
  5. Save. The dues are recorded automatically on each due date, and the pot you receive is recorded as income on the date of your turn.

Paylater (buy now, pay later)

A purchase on a paylater wallet becomes an installment automatically, and the bill is paid from another wallet.

  1. Open More › Wallet, tap Add Wallet, pick the type (or Add Manually), then turn on Paylater wallet. Fill in the Paylater limit (optional).
  2. Record the purchase as usual with that paylater wallet. Under Installment details you can pick the number of installments and the wallet that pays the bill.
  3. The purchase becomes an installment automatically, following its term. See it in More › Installments.
  4. Pay the bill: in Installments tap Pay this month's bill, then pick Pay from wallet. The money moves from that wallet to the paylater wallet.

Savings, assets & shopping

Savings goals

Set a savings target, put money aside bit by bit and watch the progress.

  1. Open More › Savings Goals, then tap add (New Savings Goal).
  2. Fill in the Goal name, the Target amount and the Target date (optional).
  3. Link a pocket so the money in it counts as your savings (More › Wallet › pick a wallet › Pocket).
  4. Follow the progress on the same page.

Assets

Record valuables or investments and follow their value over time.

  1. Open More › Assets.
  2. Tap add, enter the asset name and its value now.
  3. Update the value any time so your net worth stays right.

Shopping list

Write a shopping list, tick what you bought, then record it as a transaction.

  1. Open More › Shopping List, then tap + and pick New list (enter the List name).
  2. Tap Add item… and fill in the items. You can enter prices for an estimated total.
  3. Tick the items that are in the basket while you shop.
  4. Tap Finish shopping, then choose Record as 1 transaction or Scan receipt.

Item usage

Record items used up or thrown away to see how fast your shopping is used.

  1. Open More › Item usage.
  2. Search or add an item, then tap it to record Used up. Pick Wasted or Given away when it was not used up.
  3. See Summary for which items run out fast and how much was wasted this month.

Reports

Reports

See money in and out by category and month, with charts and comparisons.

  1. Open the Reports tab.
  2. Pick the period (month, year, all, or custom).
  3. Tap a category or wallet to see its transactions, or export the report.

Sharing with others

Split a bill

Split a shared bill between friends, work out each share and record yours.

  1. Open More › Split Bill.
  2. Enter the name (e.g. Dinner), the Bill total, the wallet that paid and the category.
  3. Tap Add person for each friend, then pick Split equally or fill in each share. Turn on I am part of the split if you ate too.
  4. Save. Your share is recorded as an expense, your friends' shares become receivables under Debts & Receivables.

Shared wallets

One wallet shared by two people or a family, every member can record.

  1. Open More › Shared Wallet, then tap Create Shared Wallet.
  2. Pick the wallet to share and name it, then Make Shared Wallet.
  3. Invite your contacts from the wallet page. Members see the balance and transactions, the notes stay on each phone.

Invite people to a Shared Wallet

Bring friends or family into one shared wallet with an invitation.

  1. Connect your friend's account first in More › Contacts.
  2. Open More › Shared Wallet › the wallet, then invite your contact.
  3. Your friend opens Shared Wallet, sees Invitation for you, and taps Join.

Contacts

Keep friends you often send to or bill, so they are quick to pick.

  1. Open More › Contacts.
  2. Tap add and send an invitation to your friend, or accept one that came in.
  3. Once connected you can send money to each other and make a Shared Wallet.

Send transactions to each other

Send transaction records to a friend who also uses Artosku, and receive theirs.

  1. Connect your friend's account first in More › Contacts.
  2. Open More › Send Money, then tap Send Money or Request Money.
  3. Pick the contact, enter the amount and (to send) pick the source wallet and the category.
  4. Tap Send. The money only arrives when your friend accepts, and comes back if declined.

Backup & restore

Google Drive backup

Your data stays on your phone and can be backed up to Google Drive for a new phone.

  1. Open More › Google Drive Backup, then tap Connect and pick the Google account. The link is remembered: signing out and back in to the same account does not need connecting again.
  2. Tap Back up now (or turn on Automatic backup) and pick the slot. Every profile is included: Personal and business. The Backup button on Home is always for backing up, never for restoring.
  3. To restore, tap Restore data (the screen is red), pick the backup, read the comparison of the backup and the data on the phone (including how many transactions would be lost), then tap Continue restoring. Want a backup first? Tap Back up first.
  4. Last confirmation: type PULIHKAN, leave Save a copy of the current data first ticked, then tap Restore now. The data on the phone is replaced completely, not merged. After Connect, a restore is only offered right away on a phone that is still empty (a new phone).
  5. Restored by mistake? On Home there is a Data restored card: tap Undo restore then Revert (it is also in More › Google Drive Backup). It works for up to 7 days. Transactions recorded after the restore are lost too.

Automatic backup to Google Drive

Turn on Automatic backup, connect or disconnect the Google Drive account, and restore from an old backup.

  1. Open More › Google Drive backup, then tap Connect and pick the Google account.
  2. Turn on Automatic backup. Tap Back up now any time for a manual backup.
  3. To restore, tap Restore data and pick the backup (Automatic backup or a Slot). You see the backup and the data on the phone side by side, how many transactions would be lost, then you have to type PULIHKAN.
  4. Restored by mistake? Tap Undo restore (the card on Home or on this screen), then Revert. The copy of the data from before the restore is kept for 7 days.
  5. Tap Switch account to use another Drive account, or Disconnect to cut Google Drive off (the backup files on Drive are not deleted).

Excel export & import

Save transactions to an Excel file, or bring them in from Excel.

  1. Open More › Excel Export & Import.
  2. Tap Back up now to make the Excel file. It holds only the profile in use (Personal or one business).
  3. To restore, tap Restore and pick the file. You are told how many transactions would be lost, then you have to type PULIHKAN. Only the data of the profile in use is replaced, and a copy of the data before is kept for 7 days (Undo restore on Home).

Automatic Excel backup to the Download folder

Save an Excel backup by itself to the phone Download folder, daily, weekly or monthly.

  1. Open More › Export & Import Excel.
  2. Turn on Save Excel to Download folder.
  3. Turn on Automatic Excel backup and pick Manual, Daily, Weekly or Monthly. The file lands in the phone Download folder.

Import an older Excel file or restore from Excel

Restore everything from an Excel backup file, or add data from an older Excel file.

  1. Open More › Export & Import Excel.
  2. Tap Restore and pick the Excel backup file: the data of the active profile is replaced. You are told how many transactions would be lost, then type PULIHKAN. A copy of the data before is kept for 7 days (Undo restore on Home).
  3. For an older Excel file, pick it and tap Add Data: the data is added and what is there stays.

AI Chat & AI

Ask Artosku AI

Ask about your money or how to use Artosku. The AI can also prepare transactions for you to approve.

  1. Tap the floating AI button.
  2. Type or say your question, e.g. "how much did I spend this month?" or "how do I make an arisan".
  3. The AI answers from your data or explains a feature, and can prepare a transaction for you to approve first.

AI usage & vouchers

See how much AI quota is left today and your extra vouchers.

  1. Open More › AI Usage.
  2. See how much of today's AI allowance is left and when it resets.
  3. Have a voucher? Enter its code on the same page.

AI consent (allow or withdraw)

Allow or withdraw the AI features. When withdrawn, the AI features stop until you allow them again; the rest is not affected.

  1. Open More › AI consent.
  2. Turn Allow AI features on or off (AI recording, AI chat, receipt scan, statement upload, category icon suggestions).
  3. When off, the AI features stop until you allow them again. The rest is not affected.

Streak & mascot

Streak & your mascot

Record every day to keep your streak, open gift boxes and look after your mascot's home.

  1. Record at least one transaction each day to keep your streak.
  2. Open More › Streak to see your streak, gift boxes and your mascot's home.
  3. Collect coins and spend them in the Shop.
  4. Freezes: every 7 days in a row you get +1 freeze, good for 14 days from the day you get it (an unused one is gone). If you miss a day, one freeze is used automatically (the one that runs out first) and the streak keeps going. With no active freeze, the streak breaks.
  5. See your active freezes and how long each one lasts in More › Streak, the Freeze section.

Mascot item shop

Trade streak coins for hats, accessories and decorations for your mascot.

  1. Open More › Streak › Shop.
  2. Pick an item (hat, accessory, decoration) and trade it for streak coins.
  3. Dress your mascot with it from Collection.

Mascot collection

See every item you own and dress your mascot with it.

  1. Open More › Streak › Collection.
  2. See every item you own.
  3. Tap an item to dress your mascot with it.

Settings & account

App lock (PIN & biometrics)

Lock Artosku with a PIN, fingerprint or face so nobody else opens your records.

  1. Open More › App Lock (PIN).
  2. Tap Set PIN, create a new PIN, then confirm it.
  3. Turn on Unlock with Biometrics to use your fingerprint or face.
  4. To change the PIN tap App Lock (PIN) again. To switch it off, tap Turn off PIN.

Appearance, language & theme

Switch between Indonesian and English, text size, and dark or light mode.

  1. Open More › Appearance.
  2. Pick the Display Mode (System, Light or Dark) and the Colour Theme.
  3. Drag Text Size to make the text bigger or smaller.
  4. Pick the language (Indonesian or English) under Language. The Arrange Home row on this page opens the Home settings.

Arrange Home

Choose which parts of Home show and put them in your order.

  1. Open More › Arrange Home.
  2. Hold and drag the Home sections to reorder them.
  3. Turn off the sections you do not want to see. Tap Restore defaults to bring back the original order.
  4. You can also open it from More › Appearance › Arrange Home. Each profile (Personal or a business) has its own arrangement.

Phone home-screen widget

Put an Artosku widget on your phone screen to see a summary and record fast.

  1. Hold your phone's home screen and pick Widgets.
  2. Find Artosku and pick the widget you want.
  3. Place it on the home screen. Tap it to record straight away or open Artosku.

Pro & Max plans

A bigger AI quota and Business Mode. Pick the plan that suits you.

Announcements

News and outage notices from the Artosku team.

  1. Open More › Announcements.

Guide center

Short guides for each menu that you can replay any time.

  1. Open More › Guide Center.

Treat us a matcha

If Artosku is useful, you can treat the team to a matcha.

Switch account

Switch between the accounts saved on this phone without signing in again.

  1. Open More › Switch profile.
  2. Tap Switch account and pick a saved account, or add another one.
  3. Each account has its own data on this phone.

Privacy policy & terms

Read how your data is protected and the rules of using Artosku.

  1. Open More › Privacy Policy to read how your data is protected.
  2. The Terms and Conditions are in the same menu, under Help & Info.

Unlock with fingerprint or face

Once a PIN is set, open Artosku with just your fingerprint or face.

  1. Set a PIN first under More › App Lock (PIN) › Set PIN.
  2. Once the PIN is on, the Unlock with Biometrics switch appears under it. Turn it on.
  3. The switch only appears when your phone has a fingerprint or face unlock.

Turn off the PIN or app lock

Remove the PIN so Artosku opens without a lock again.

  1. Open More. The red Turn off PIN button is under App Lock (PIN), only while a PIN is on.
  2. Tap Turn off PIN. Artosku opens without a lock again.
  3. To change the PIN, tap App Lock (PIN) and make a new one.

Account password (change or add)

Change your account password, or add one if you signed in with Google so you can sign in with email too.

  1. Open More, then tap Change Password. If you signed in with Google and have none yet, the row is called Add Password.
  2. Fill in the New password (at least 6 characters) and Confirm password.
  3. Tap Save.

Change your name and profile photo

Change your nickname, pick a profile photo, or hide the name and email on the profile card.

  1. Open More. On the profile card at the top tap the pencil icon (Rename), or Add Name if there is none.
  2. Write your Nickname and tap Save.
  3. For the photo, tap it and pick a picture. Tap Hide to hide the name and email (View profile to show them again).

Sign out

Sign out of your Artosku account on this phone.

  1. Open More, scroll down, then tap Sign out.
  2. Your transactions stay on this phone. To switch to another account without signing out, use Switch account.

Start over (delete all data, keep the account)

Delete all transactions and financial data in this account and start from empty. The account stays.

  1. Open More, scroll down, then tap Reset Account and press and hold Hold to Reset Account until it finishes (Keep holding...).
  2. All transactions, wallets, categories, budgets, assets and debts in this account are deleted for good. The account stays.
  3. If unsure, make a Google Drive backup first.

Delete account

Delete your account and all its data from this phone for good.

  1. Open More, scroll down, then tap Delete Account.
  2. Type HAPUS to confirm, then tap Delete Account.
  3. The local data of this account is deleted too and cannot be brought back. To only empty the data, use Reset Account.

Search the menu in More

Find a menu by typing its name. If nothing is found, a button asks Artosku AI instead.

  1. In More tap the magnifier icon at the top right.
  2. Type the name of the menu, e.g. pin or payday. The list narrows as you type.
  3. If nothing is found, tap Ask Artosku AI.

Manage, cancel or restore a subscription

See your plan, switch between monthly and yearly, manage it in the Play Store, and restore purchases.

  1. Open More › Subscription. Current plan shows your plan.
  2. Pick Monthly or Yearly, then tap the plan to move up.
  3. Tap Manage subscription to cancel or change it in the Play Store. After cancelling the plan stays active until it runs out.
  4. New phone or purchase not read? Tap Restore purchases.

Replay all guides, or turn off the guides that appear by themselves when a menu opens.

Handbook (full tutorials)

Full tutorials for every feature on the website, with a search box. Opens in the browser.

  1. Open More › Handbook. The website opens in the browser.

Quick how-tos for common goals

A weekly allowance

  1. On the Home, on the Allowance card, tap Weekly beside "Allowance per day" (or the Daily allowance ▾ tab › Show per week).
  2. This week's allowance is the money available divided by the days left to payday, for the days of that week (Monday to Sunday). The last week before payday counts only the days that are left.
  3. The card shows Left this week, Spent, and Safe per day. Tap Daily to go back; the choice is kept for each allowance.
  4. Want a weekly limit per category (e.g. snacks)? More › Budget › the Weekly tab.

A weekly budget for one category

  1. Open More › Budget, then tap the + button in the header.
  2. Pick the Category, then Period: Weekly. Fill in Limit per week (a week counts Monday to Sunday) and Save.
  3. See it in the Weekly tab; the › arrow goes to next week (up to 8 weeks). Copy last week is the copy icon in the title.
  4. A category can have a monthly AND a weekly limit; they stand apart and are never added. You are reminded at 90% and when it is passed.

Limit snacks per day, different on the weekend

  1. Open More › Budget, then tap the + button in the header.
  2. Pick the Category (e.g. Snacks), then Period: Daily. Fill in Limit per day (general, all days), e.g. 20,000.
  3. Tap + By day, pick Sat and Sun, fill in the weekend limit (or turn on No limit), then Save. Just one date? + By date.
  4. Tap Save in the form. What is used: date > day > general. See it in the Daily tab or on the Today's budget card on Home; you are reminded once when the limit of that day is passed.

A big purchase should not use up today's allowance

  1. Record the purchase as usual first, so your wallet balance stays right.
  2. Tap the transaction (on Home or in History), then tap Leave out of allowance.
  3. Pick How to count: Spread over the days left (the default, good for a big purchase now and then) then tap Leave out.
  4. Today's allowance is not used up at once. Your balance did go down, so the allowance of the days until payday goes down a little. Want it as if it never happened? Pick Ignore completely, but the allowance can then be bigger than the money you have.
  5. Changed your mind? In the transaction detail tap Undo exclusion.

Monthly pocket money for a child or partner

  1. Open More › Recurring Transactions, then tap Add recurring transaction.
  2. Pick Expense, fill in the Amount, the source wallet and a Category (e.g. Pocket money). Add a Note, e.g. "Pocket money for Rani".
  3. Pick Frequency Monthly (or Weekly), set Start from to the day you usually give it, and Remind how many days before.
  4. Save. Artosku records it automatically on each due date and reminds you. You still hand over the money yourself.

Saving every payday

  1. Make the goal: More › Savings Goals, tap add (New Savings Goal). Fill in the Goal name, the Target amount and the Target date. Artosku works out "Set aside ≈ … per month".
  2. Set a fixed reminder: More › Recurring Transactions › add, an Expense of that amount with a savings Category, Frequency Monthly, Start from your payday.
  3. When the reminder comes, move the money to your savings account or pocket, then tap Save in Savings Goals.
  4. Note: Artosku cannot move money between wallets by itself yet; only the recording and the reminder are automatic.

Paying rent every month

  1. Open More › Recurring Transactions, then tap Add recurring transaction.
  2. Pick Expense, fill in the Amount, the wallet that pays and a Category (e.g. Rent). Add a Note, e.g. "Room rent".
  3. Pick Frequency Monthly and set Start from to the payment day (e.g. the 1st). Slide Remind how many days before.
  4. Save. Each month the payment is recorded automatically on that day, and you are reminded beforehand.

Recording your salary automatically

  1. Open More › Recurring Transactions, then tap Add recurring transaction.
  2. Pick Income, fill in your salary Amount, the wallet that receives it and the Salary Category.

What the Finance cards mean (margin, break-even, cash lasts for) Pro

What Business cash now, Cash lasts for, Gross margin, Net margin, Break-even and Cost / sales mean.

  1. Open Reports › Finance and tap a card; its explanation shows, tap Got it to close.
  2. Business cash now: the sum of the balances of all your business wallets (paylater wallets are not counted).
  3. Cash lasts for: business cash divided by the average monthly cost of the 3 months before, i.e. how many months it lasts with no sales at all.
  4. Gross margin: of each Rp100 of sales, what is left after the cost of goods. Net margin: how much becomes profit after all costs.
  5. Break-even: the least sales in a month so you do not lose. Cost / sales: what share of sales goes on cost of goods and costs.

Why sales differ from money received Pro

Reports count money that really came in and went out. Unpaid receipts, owner draws and capital deposits are left out.

  1. Reports are counted from money that has come in and gone out, not from receipts alone.
  2. An unpaid receipt is counted only when its payment is received; the rest sits under Receivables.
  3. Owner draws, capital deposits, opening balances and moves between businesses are not counted as income or cost.
  4. Check Reports › Profit & loss: Gross sales, Platform fees and "Other income and receivable payments" explain the difference.

  2. Pick Between business wallets or To another business, then the source wallet and the target wallet (or your other business).
  3. The admin fee, if any, is recorded as a cost. The move is not counted as sales.

Combined summary (Personal + all businesses) Pro

See your personal money and all your businesses side by side.

  1. Open More › Combined summary.
  2. See your Personal money and all your businesses side by side; it is only for looking.

Create a new business and switch profile Pro

More than one business? Create a new one and switch between Personal and your businesses.

  1. Tap the profile pill at the top of the Home (or More › Switch profile).
  2. Pick Personal, Combined, or one of your businesses. Tap New business to create another one.
  3. To rename or delete a business, use Rename or Delete business in that list.

Making your first sale Pro

  1. Open More › Switch profile, then create a new business (or pick yours) and follow the setup.
  2. Add products: More › Products and Stock › Add product (name, selling price, cost, stock if tracked).
  3. On the Business Home tap Sell, tap the product (or Custom item), then Pay and pick Cash, Transfer or QRIS.
  4. On the Sale saved screen: Invoice, WA to send it to the buyer, or Sell again.

Recording customer credit and collecting it Pro

  1. In Sell, fill the basket then tap Pay and pick Pay later. Fill in the customer name (required for unpaid sales).
  2. Save. The sale becomes a receivable. See them all in the Sales tab › Not fully paid.
  3. To collect, tap the invoice and send it through WhatsApp.
  4. When the money arrives, receive the payment: fill in Received now or Pay in full, pick Goes into the wallet, then save.

Closing the till every day Pro

  1. Open More › Business Settings › Cashier recap, then tap Close the till under the recap.
  2. See what should be in the drawer (today's cash sales), then count the money in it (by denomination if you like).
  3. If there is a Difference, tap Book as a difference. If it is Exact, you are done.
  4. See today's result in the Sales tab and the Business Home.

Recording employee pay Pro

  1. A fixed monthly cost: More › Recurring Transactions › add, Expense, the salary Amount, the business wallet, Category Employee Pay, Frequency Monthly, Start from payday.
  2. Or record it once: Business Home › Purchases, pick Costs, fill in the pay, then Pay.
  3. To lend money to an employee: More › Business Settings › Lend money.
  4. See the effect on profit in the Reports tab › profit and loss.

Taking business money for yourself Pro

  1. Open More › Owner's Money.
  2. Pick To personal, then the source business wallet and the personal wallet it goes to.
  3. Enter the amount (Artosku shows this month's profit and what stays in the business), then save.
  4. It is recorded as a draw in the business and as income in Personal at once, not as a cost.

Checking this month's profit Pro

  1. Open the Reports tab in Business Mode.
  2. Pick the profit and loss tab and the period (this month).
  3. See turnover, costs and profit. Tap Export to share the report.
  4. Quickly without opening the report: the Business Home shows today's profit, and More › Business Settings › Monthly target helps follow turnover.

Sending a receipt with your QRIS on it Pro

  1. Open More › Business Settings › Receipt & ways to pay. Under Ways to pay tap Add, pick QRIS, then Pick QRIS picture from the gallery. Tap Save.
  2. Make the sale from the Business Home › Sell.
  3. Open the Sales tab, tap the receipt, then tap Receipt.
  4. Send it as PDF, Photo (a PNG image, its QR is big enough to scan) or WA (text). To change the QRIS picture: tap the QRIS row in Receipt & ways to pay › Change picture.

Recording ingredient purchases and seeing the COGS Pro

  1. On the Business Home open Purchases, pick Ingredients or Stock for sale, add the items and their prices, then Pay and Save.
  2. Ingredient and stock purchases go in the report as Cost of goods (COGS). Which categories count as COGS is set under More › Business Settings › Categories & materials (tap a category label to move it between cost of goods and operating).
  3. See the COGS in Reports › Profit & loss (the Cost of goods row; tap it to see its categories, tap a category to see its notes).

Taking an order for a certain date with a deposit Pro

  1. On the Business Home tap Sell, pick the products, tap Pay, then Order picked up later (pre-order).
  2. Fill in the pickup date and time, Pickup or Delivery, the deposit (e.g. 50%), the Buyer name and the Order note (e.g. what is written on the cake). Tap Save order · deposit.
  3. See the date under More › Pre-order › Calendar; Recap to make shows what has to be made.
  4. At pickup open the order and tap Done · receive the rest. The receipt is made on the pickup day and the deposit becomes part of it.

Recording a business cost and seeing where it goes Pro

  1. Tap the Catat button under the + button (or + › Pay a cost), pick Expense, fill in the amount and category, then save.
  2. Under the category a line says "Goes in the report as ..." (COGS, Operating cost, or Not counted in profit).
  3. After saving you are taken to Sales › Notes. Tap the note to change or delete it.
