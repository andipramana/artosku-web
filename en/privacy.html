Privacy Policy

Artosku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Artosku is a personal finance tracking app. This policy explains the data stored on your device, the data sent when you choose certain features, and the deletion options available to you.

In short

By default, your main financial records and receipt photos are stored in the app's space on your device. Artosku does not keep a routine copy of those records on the app's servers. The exception is the features that connect you with other users (Contacts, Send Money, money requests, and shared wallets): some of their data is stored on the server so it can be shared, as described below. Some optional features, such as Google Drive backup and AI recording, also require sending data, as described below.

Account and authentication data

If you sign up with email, our authentication system processes your email address and password to create and secure your account. Artosku cannot read your password in plain text. If you choose to log in with Google, Google and our system process the account information needed for authentication, such as your email and account identifier.

Data stored on your device

  • Financial records — transactions, wallets, categories, budgets, assets, debts and receivables, savings goals, recurring transactions, and related data are stored in the app's local database.
  • Receipt photos — if you choose to attach them, the photos are stored in the app's storage space on your device.
  • PIN and biometrics — your actual PIN is never stored or sent to a server; the app stores a hash of the PIN together with a salt in the device's secure storage. Your fingerprint or face is processed by the operating system; Artosku only receives a success or failure result.

Local data can be used for recording once an account session is available. Additional protection against a lost device or access by other people also depends on the security of your device.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive and create a backup, Artosku requests limited access to the Artosku app data folder (appdata) in your Google account. This folder is hidden and can only be accessed through that Google account. A backup may contain local records and receipt photos. Artosku does not add its own encryption to backup files; their confidentiality depends on the security of the Google account you connect, so make sure that account uses a strong password and 2-step verification. A new backup replaces the previous Artosku backup file. Google Drive can only be connected with the same Google account you use to log in to Artosku.

Deleting your Artosku account does not automatically delete your backup in Google Drive. You need to delete the Artosku app data from your Google Drive settings separately.

AI recording feature (optional)

When you knowingly run AI recording, Artosku sends the transaction sentence, the detected amount, the wallet name, and the names and types of the available categories to the Artosku backend. The backend passes the necessary information on to DeepSeek, a third-party AI service provider that processes it outside Indonesia, to produce a transaction suggestion. The AI feature is optional, and we ask for your consent separately before it is used for the first time. You can withdraw that consent at any time. Our server logs every AI request to control costs and usage limits: account and device identifiers, feature type, token count, cost, time, and text length. The content of the sentence you send and the content of the AI's reply are not stored in that log. For usage limits, the account identifier, installation identifier, usage count, and period time may be processed/stored. Your local financial records are not uploaded in full; do not enter information you do not want to send to that service.

Business Mode (Pro and Max)

Business Mode (for Pro and Max subscribers only) stores the products, prices, sales, invoices, and customer names and phone numbers you enter, only on your device and separate from your personal records. Like your other records, this data is included in Google Drive backups and in the Excel files you run yourself. The logo, payment QR code, and account number you put on invoices are stored only on your device and are printed on the invoices you share. Artosku does not send messages to customers: the WhatsApp button only opens WhatsApp on your device with the text already filled in, and you are the one who sends it. You are responsible for the customer data you store. Cashier Recap creates a file containing invoices (including customer names and numbers) that you share yourself through the app of your choice; Artosku does not send it anywhere. If you use "Fill with AI" to record a sale, the sales sentence you type or speak and the names and prices of your business products are sent in the same way as AI recording above; a customer name is only sent if you mention it in that sentence, and the customer list is not sent.

Capture from notifications (optional, Android)

If you turn it on, Artosku reads notifications from the bank and digital wallet apps you choose in order to turn them into transaction drafts. Notification access permission is granted through Android settings and you can revoke it at any time.

What is stored on your device: the app name, the title and content of notifications from the apps you choose, their time, and the amount, type, and party read from them. Notifications from other apps are not read and not stored.

This data is processed only on your device. It is not sent to Artosku servers, and it is not included in Google Drive backups or automatic Excel files. There is one exception, and it is in your hands: if you tap "Tidy up with AI" on a draft and consent to it, one short line per draft (type, amount, party name without long numbers, and wallet name) is sent to the AI feature as with Record with AI. The notification content itself is not sent.

Drafts that you do not save or that you cancel are deleted automatically after 60 days. You can also delete them all through the Notification Capture menu, and logging out of your account deletes the notification queue on your device.

Contacts, Send Money, and shared wallets

These features store some data on our servers so it can be shared with the people you connect with:

  • Contacts and invite codes — display name, public key, the relationship between users, and invite codes along with their validity period.
  • Send Money and money requests — amount, currency, status, time, and who the sender and recipient are. No money moves through Artosku; this is record-keeping only.
  • Shared wallets — members, wallet settings (currency, color, icon), and the transactions that members record: amount, type, category name, time, and who recorded them.

The shared wallet name and transaction notes are encrypted on your device before they are sent, and we do not store the key. Amounts, categories, and times are stored on the server without additional encryption so that the shared balance can be calculated. All members of a shared wallet can see the transactions in that wallet. Invite only people you trust.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, a device token is stored on our servers and used to send notifications through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, for example for invitations or shared wallet activity.

Device identifier and vouchers

The app creates a random identifier for your device. This identifier is used to limit AI and receipt scanning usage and to prevent vouchers from being claimed repeatedly. It may remain after you log out or delete your account, and it disappears when the app is removed from your device. Streak, coins, and mascot item data are calculated and stored on your device; the server only records the vouchers you claim, along with the account and device identifier.

Subscription

When subscriptions are available, payments are processed by Google Play. Artosku does not receive your card number or payment data; we only receive the subscription status (plan, active period, and cancellation status).

Consent records

When you agree to the Terms and Conditions, the Privacy Policy, or the AI feature (and when you withdraw that agreement), we record your account, device identifier, the version of the text you agreed to, and the time. We keep this record as proof of consent.

Crash reports and technical data

In non-debug versions of the app, Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash/error reports, stack traces, the app version, device and operating system information, and technical context. Artosku does not intentionally add the contents of the transaction database as Crashlytics attributes, but error messages may contain incidental information. This data is used to diagnose and fix the app.

Website

The contact form on the website only opens the email app on your device with the message already filled in; what you write in the form is not sent to or stored on our servers, and it is only sent if you yourself tap send in the email app. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads Google Fonts; GitHub and Google may receive technical request data such as your IP address and browser information when the page loads. Questions about the website can be sent to contact.artosku@gmail.com.

Service providers and data sharing

Artosku does not sell or rent your data and does not use ad SDKs or Firebase Analytics. Data is only sent to service providers to the extent needed for the features you choose: our backend service provider for authentication and AI processing; the third-party AI service provider for AI requests; and Google for Google Sign-In, Google Drive, Google Fonts, and Firebase Crashlytics. These providers may process technical data such as IP address, user-agent, request time, and service identifiers according to their own policies.

Delete your account and data

In the app, go to More → Delete Account and follow the confirmation. Artosku deletes the local records and receipt photos of the active account from your device, then asks our server to delete the authentication account; the server step requires an internet connection. If the server step fails, the server account may still exist even though the local data has already been deleted.

This deletion does not automatically delete your Google Drive backup, your PIN/local settings, or the AI usage records needed for service limits. Other local data can be deleted through the app/device settings. If you cannot open the app, send a request from your registered account email to contact.artosku@gmail.com; we can delete the service account, but we cannot delete data that exists only on your device or in your own Google Drive.

Delete part of your data

You can delete transactions, wallets, categories, assets, budgets, and receipt photos from the relevant screen. Local deletion does not retroactively change backups that have already been made; the next backup will replace the old backup with the latest state of your local data.

Data transfers abroad

The service providers we use (the backend, Google, and DeepSeek) may process data outside Indonesia. For the AI feature, we ask for your consent separately before data is sent.

Users under 18

The minimum age to use the app is 13. Users under 18 need permission from a parent or guardian. We do not sell user data, and we do not use it for advertising or commercial profiling, regardless of age. A parent or guardian can request deletion of a child's account and data by email at the address below.

Your rights over your data

You have the right to request information about your data, to access and correct it, to delete it, to restrict its processing, and to withdraw any consent you have given. Send your request to contact.artosku@gmail.com from your account email. For withdrawal of consent, we stop processing within the time set by the Personal Data Protection Law (3 x 24 hours from receiving the request). Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that has already taken place.

In case of a data breach

If a personal data protection failure occurs, we notify affected users and the competent authority in writing within 3 x 24 hours of becoming aware of the failure, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when our features or data processing change. Privacy questions can be sent to contact.artosku@gmail.com.
